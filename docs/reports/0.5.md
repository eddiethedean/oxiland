## Claim

Oxiland 0.5 documents a shared fallible-iterator stream policy (ADR-013), ships
URI/file/Unicode/digest/namespace/vocabulary helpers under `oxiland::utility`,
adds `World` logging with optional `tracing` (ADR-014–ADR-015), and dispositions
Redland hashes/lists as standard Rust collections (ADR-016). Compatibility is
scoped to the curated 0.5 inventory slice and the tests linked from it.

## Not claimed

- Full Redland symbol accounting / `rdfproc` (0.6)
- C ABI stream handles (0.8)
- Unifying stream trait or lending iterators
- Parse/load wall-clock timeout
- Additional digest algorithms beyond md5/sha1/sha256
